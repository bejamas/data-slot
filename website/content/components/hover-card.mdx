## Anatomy

A trigger and its content. The card opens on hover and on keyboard focus after a short delay, so keep the trigger focusable.

```html
<div data-slot="hover-card">
  <button data-slot="hover-card-trigger">Trigger</button>
  <div data-slot="hover-card-content">Content</div>
</div>
```
