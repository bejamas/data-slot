## Anatomy

One `accordion-item` per section, each with its own `data-value` — items without one are skipped. Inside an item, the trigger and content are wired together for you. `accordion-trigger-icon` and `accordion-content-inner` are optional styling hooks.

```html
<div data-slot="accordion">
  <div data-slot="accordion-item" data-value="unique-id">
    <button data-slot="accordion-trigger">
      <span>Trigger</span>
      <span data-slot="accordion-trigger-icon">+</span>
    </button>
    <div data-slot="accordion-content">
      <div data-slot="accordion-content-inner">Content</div>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>
```
